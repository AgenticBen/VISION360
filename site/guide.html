How to use VISION360

A step-by-step guide to the vision loss services directory, the voice and chat assistant, and the staff queue. Every part works with a keyboard and a screen reader, and nothing here needs an account.

1. Getting around any page

  • Skip links. Press Tab once when a page opens to reveal links that jump straight to search, the three views, the list, the filters, or the What's new tour.
  • Moving around. Tab moves forward, Shift + Tab moves back, Enter or Space presses a button, and Escape closes any pop-up window.
  • Make it easier to read. At the top of the directory, choose a text size of 100%, 125% or 150%, or turn on High contrast. Your browser's own zoom also works. In the assistant, the Settings button offers text up to 200% and dark or high-contrast colors.
  • Screen readers. Pages use real headings, lists and buttons. New messages from the assistant are announced once, when they are complete.

2. The directory

The directory lists every organization we know of that helps people who are blind, have low vision, or are losing their sight in the greater Charlotte area.

Search

  1. Type in the box at the top: an organization, a service such as braille or rides, a county, or a need.
  2. Press Search directory. The list below shows only matching organizations and says how many matched.
  3. Press Clear search to see everything again.

Filter and sort the list

  • Use the Filters to narrow by category, area or how well a listing is confirmed. Clear all removes every filter.
  • Use Sort to change the order. Mecklenburg first groups organizations by how close they are; you can also sort by name or by confirmation.
  • Nothing is ever hidden for being far away. Distance only changes the order, so a service an hour away is still in the list, clearly labeled.

Open a listing

  1. Press an organization's name to expand it.
  2. You'll see what they do, who qualifies, cost, documents to bring, every referral step, and how to contact them. Phone numbers and email addresses are links.
  3. Each listing has a Copy link to this organization button so you can share exactly that organization.
  4. A yellow Not published — verification call required label means we haven't confirmed that detail yet; it tells you what to ask when you call.

Tell us what to improve

The Help improve this site button at the bottom opens a short form. It hands your note to your own email app; you press Send there. Feedback is reviewed before any listing changes.

3. Find services (a few questions)

  1. Choose the Find services view near the top of the directory.
  2. Answer one question at a time. You can choose more than one answer where it says so, or press Skip this question.
  3. Press Stop and show results now at any point. You don't have to answer everything.
  4. Results show the best matches first, each with the reasons it was suggested. Show the other … reveals the rest, in the same order.
  5. If you mention something the directory doesn't cover, such as housing or food, you'll see where to turn instead.

4. Pathways

The Pathways view shows common routes through services, step by step, with every step written out in text as well as drawn.

5. The voice and chat assistant

This is a demonstration. Please use made-up details, not a real person's health or contact information.

Start

  1. Open the assistant. Read the short notes, then choose Talk (uses your microphone) or Type (works everywhere).
  2. Use Settings in the top-right corner any time for text size, colors, read-aloud and voice speed.
  3. You can switch between talking and typing without losing anything.

Answer the questions

  • The assistant asks one question at a time. Answer in your own words.
  • You can say or type: repeat, skip, I don't know, change my county, or talk to a person.
  • Not sure or no idea is recorded as not sure, never as yes or no. If an age or date is unclear, the assistant asks a short follow-up instead of guessing.

Check the summary

  1. Before suggesting anything, the assistant reads back what it heard.
  2. If it's right, say or press yes. Only a clear yes confirms it.
  3. If something is wrong, use a Change button, or just say it: it's for my mother, I'm 70. Saying yes, but I live in Gaston changes the answer and reads the summary again; it does not count as a yes.

Your suggestions

  • You'll get up to eight services, each with why it fits, how to start, and contacts. Ask tell me about number three to hear more.
  • To get the plan by email, say yes when asked, give an address, check it when it's read back, then give a clear yes to send. In this demo, emails go to a test outbox.
  • To ask Lions Services staff to follow up, say yes when offered and choose how they should contact you.
  • End session is always there and turns the microphone off. In voice mode, Stop speaking and Repeat last response are there too.

6. The staff queue (Lions Services staff)

  1. Open the staff queue and sign in with your staff account.
  2. The list shows the newest conversations first: their stage, staff status, anything needing attention, and email status.
  3. Open a conversation to see confirmed and unconfirmed answers, every suggestion with its reasons and the data version behind it, emails, and follow-up requests.
  4. Mark it reviewed or needing follow-up, or retry an email that failed. A status change never changes the person's answers.

7. The What's new tour

  • On the directory, the What's new button is in the bottom-left corner (it reads New on small screens). On the assistant and the staff queue, it's in the top-right corner next to Settings.
  • It opens a short tour of that page. Use Next and Back to move through the steps.
  • Show me closes the tour, takes you to the part being described and outlines it. Press Tab once to reach Back to the tour.
  • Recent changes lists what has improved lately. A dot on the button means there's something you haven't seen.

8. Privacy and what is saved

  • The directory doesn't save your searches or answers. Display settings are remembered on this device only.
  • The assistant keeps structured answers, not full transcripts, for up to 7 days so staff can follow up if you ask. This demo is for made-up details only.
  • Speech recognition in Chrome and Edge is handled by the browser maker's service; Safari uses Apple's. Choose Type to avoid that.
