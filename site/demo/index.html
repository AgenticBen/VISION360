Before you start

  • This is a demonstration. Please don't use a real person's health or personal details.
  • An automated assistant, not a person, will ask a few questions one at a time, then suggest services from the Lions Services directory. It checks everything with you before suggesting anything.
  • The assistant will ask your first name, so a staff member knows who they're helping. Your answers are kept on the demo server for up to 7 days so staff can follow up if you ask. The full conversation isn't saved there, and this site doesn't record audio.
  • If you choose Talk: your browser turns speech into text. In Chrome and Edge, the browser sends your voice to its maker's speech service to do that; Safari uses Apple's. Choose Type to avoid that.
  • You can change the text size, colors and voice options in Settings at any time.
