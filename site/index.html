Your connection to vision services

Vision 360 helps people in the Charlotte area who are blind, have low vision, or are losing their sight, and the family and professionals who support them. Talk or type, one question at a time, and find the right next step.

Vision Assessment A five-minute check of how your vision affects everyday things, like reading, faces and getting around. It isn't a medical exam. Start the assessment → Resource Assistant Answer a few questions by talking or typing, and get suggestions for services and programs near you, with the reason for each one. Find resources →

How to use it

  1. Pick a tool. The Vision Assessment looks at how vision affects daily life. The Resource Assistant finds services that can help.
  2. Talk or type. Press Talk and speak, or press Type and write. On a computer you can just press the Space bar to start talking.
  3. Answer one question at a time. Say "skip", "I don't know" or "repeat" whenever you like. Nothing is sent or suggested until you've checked your answers.
  4. Make it comfortable. Say "bigger text", "smaller text" or "less contrast" at any time, or use Display settings at the top of the page.

Why Vision 360 exists

Finding help after vision loss can mean many phone calls to agencies, clinics and programs, each with its own rules. Vision 360 brings those services together in one place, in a form anyone can use by voice or by keyboard, so people can find the right next step sooner and with more confidence.

It's built by Lions Services, which has served people who are blind or have low vision in Charlotte since 1935. A real person at Lions Services can always follow up.
